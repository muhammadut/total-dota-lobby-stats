Rating measures how far a record goes towards proving its owner is better than a coin flip — which is the only thing that means anything when every match makes exactly five winners and five losers. 100 is that proof; a 1–0 player scores about 26, because one game proves nothing however it went. Green marks the records that are most of the way there. Games count sets how much proof is demanded, and so how much simply turning up is worth — a 3–1 record ranks 6th on Less, 7th on More, 9th on Most, while a 26-game 18–8 season takes 1st on either of the stricter two.

Teams

Four teams playing continuously through Dec 31, 2026 for a $500 prize. Standings rank by the same Rating that drives the individual board — a lower-bound on team win rate, so more games at a good rate always beats fewer at a great one. Teams below 80% of the league median pace at the season's end are ineligible for the prize.

Schedule

The season runs straight through — no playoffs, no final. Two best-of-three matches a night, Saturday and Sunday: one pair of teams plays the early slot, the other the late one, so whoever isn't playing can watch. Pick your country to see every time in your own clock.

View

The late slot starts at 3:00 AM in Pakistan, which is the next morning — a Saturday-night late match is really Sunday at 3 AM, and a Sunday-night one lands on Monday. Every pair of teams meets the same number of times, and each team takes the late slot exactly half its nights. Times are worked out with full daylight-saving awareness for each country.

Coord

Match scheduling across four timezones. Confirmed matches show up in Next Match; anything a captain is currently trying to schedule shows up in Open rounds with the top three UTC slots the finder found. All availability is posted in #dota-league-2026 on Discord — this tab is read-only.

Slot ranking prefers windows where both teams have 3 or more players available (a game is playable with one stand-in). Times are computed with full DST awareness for each player's declared timezone. If a slot straddles a DST transition, the local wall-clock reflects the rule in effect on that date.

On the record

Transcribed by hand from post-game screenshots into a SQLite database, then exported here. Nothing is scraped — these are private lobby games and they do not appear in any public API.

Every match is checksummed on entry against team kills ≤ team score ≤ enemy deaths. Breaking that ordering is arithmetically impossible, so it flags a misread digit before it can skew a win rate.

Players who renamed themselves mid-session are merged by hand, never by string-matching.
