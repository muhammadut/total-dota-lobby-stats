The Ancients’ Ledger

 

Standings

Every win and loss is derived from which side the player was on — never stored twice. Sort by any column.

# Player GP W L Radiant — Dire line Win % K / D / A KDA GPM

KDA is (kills + assists) ÷ deaths; ∞ marks an undefined ratio — a player who has never died, or never lost. Rows with one or two games are noise, not skill.

Match Log

Ordered by match ID — globally sequential, and therefore the true chronology even when clocks from different clients disagree. Select a match to open its full scoreboard.

Hero Record

Every hero drafted, and how it fared. Hero names come from the scoreboard tab, which prints them as text.

On the record

Transcribed by hand from post-game screenshots into a SQLite database, then exported here. Nothing is scraped — these are private lobby games and they do not appear in any public API.

Every match is checksummed on entry against the rule team kills ≤ team score ≤ enemy deaths. A violation of that ordering is arithmetically impossible and flags a misread digit before it can skew a win rate.

Players who renamed themselves mid-session are merged by hand, never by string-matching.
